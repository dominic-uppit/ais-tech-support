## The learning path (5 courses, in order)

Source: `START HERE → Your Journey from 0 to First Client` (lesson-id: `fcd6622018ee4d7aa67778ec8599463f`)

1. **START HERE** — onboarding. Open immediately.
2. **The AI Partner Model** — the business model context. **Unlocks at Level 3** (~20 likes from other members; achievable in ~30 minutes of genuine engagement).
3. **Build Your Portfolio** — foundations + the Claude Code course. Open immediately.
4. **Get Your First Clients** — **unlocks after 30 days** of membership.
5. **Scale** — **unlocks after 90 days** of membership.

**Premium membership bypasses all gating** and unlocks everything instantly, plus perks like the $3M Savings Vault. **Annual members also get the gated courses immediately** — the START HERE lesson names only Premium, but the support team has confirmed Annual behaves the same way in multiple threads. Always ask which plan the student is on before telling them to wait out a 30/90-day gate.

When a student says "I can't access course X" — check the unlock rules above BEFORE treating it as a bug. "Why is this locked?" is usually just the 30/90-day gate or Level 3 requirement.

## How to get help (the official way)

Source: `START HERE → Getting Help with Automations` (lesson-id: `9e6dcdd305ee4e2d869c0a33027b2b7f`) — 2-minute video + guidelines.

For technical/automation problems, the official process is:

1. **Search first** — the community, n8n docs, and free forums. Many problems already have a solved thread (look for [SOLVED] in titles).
2. **Post in the "Support Needed" category** with a specific, descriptive title. "Webhook not triggering on form submit" beats "Help please!"
3. **Include screenshots or a Loom video** — without visuals, the first reply is usually "can you share a screenshot?", which just slows everything down.
4. **Reply or @mention** people so they get notified when you respond.
5. **Mark the title [SOLVED]** when resolved, so the next person with the same issue can find it.
6. **Like helpful responses.**

Tech support via Support Needed‼️ is unlimited and included — students should never feel like they're imposing by posting. Team-stated response time is typically within 24 hours (volume and time zones can stretch it). ⚠️ Do not promise a "100% resolution guarantee" — that phrasing comes from marketing copy quoted by a community member, not from any lesson, and promising it sets up a grievance the skill created.

## Billing, payments, account access

Source: `START HERE → Help With Billing & Your Account` (lesson-id: `60878a56ab794a23b53c31c45d8d6332`)

Billing is handled by **email, not community posts**: **nate@aiautomationsociety.ai**

That covers: charges/invoices/receipts, failed payments, card updates, plan changes, cancellations, login/lockout problems, refund requests. Include the email tied to the AIS+ account, a brief description, and screenshots. Response time: within ~24 hours on business days.

**Route billing questions to that email — NOT to Support Needed.** And the reverse: technical questions go to Support Needed, not the billing email.

## Community rules (summarized)

Source: `START HERE → AIS+ Community Rules` (lesson-id: `6d33ccc0efdd4c9eaa0ac6da9feb87a1`)

1. No promotions or selling (no pitching, no "DM me for work")
2. Be respectful, always
3. Keep posts relevant, valuable, and real
4. Give more than you take
5. No spam (no copy-paste posts, mass tagging, repetitive content)
6. Protect privacy — don't share private conversations, paid content, or sensitive client info
7. Title posts clearly

Rule 6 matters for support posts: remind students to **redact API keys, client names, and credentials from screenshots** before posting.

## Milestones & expectations (for "am I behind?" questions)

Source: `START HERE → Milestones Along the Way` (lesson-id: `3e801ec70ce74ee9b8cc29fce67cbf54`)

- Level 3 unlock: first 1–2 weeks of active engagement
- First working build: weeks 2–4
- First client conversation: ~month 2 (outreach materials unlock in month 2)
- First paying client: months 2–3 of consistent effort

There's no timer — these aren't deadlines. When a student sounds discouraged about pace, this lesson is the reference.

## Posting categories (verified from the live community, July 2026)

| Category | What goes there |
|---|---|
| **General discussion 💬** | Anything that doesn't fit below — intros, general questions, discussion |
| **Support Needed‼️** | Technical/automation help. Official description: "The space to get all your questions answered! Please title your question as clearly as possible." This is where the skill's support-post escape hatch points. |
| **Agency Talk 💼** | Business, agency, pricing, and client questions |
| **Collaboration/Hiring 🤝** | Showcase skills, find collaborators. NOT for promoting businesses. |
| **Wins 🌟** | Share wins, big or small |
| **Gems💎** | High-value shares/resources |
| **Announcements 📢** | Official announcements (team posts) |
| **Call Recordings 📞** | Recordings of live calls |

Routing rules for the skill:
- Tech problem the skill can't solve → **Support Needed‼️**
- Business/agency/pricing/client question → **Agency Talk 💼**
- "I want to team up with someone" → **Collaboration/Hiring 🤝**
- Student solved their problem and is proud of it → suggest sharing in **Wins 🌟**
- "Where are the live call recordings?" → **Call Recordings 📞** category + `Live Call Recordings` course in the classroom
